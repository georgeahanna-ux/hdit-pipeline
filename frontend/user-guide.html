1. Welcome to Pipeline

Pipeline is a simple, visual board for the opportunities in your Autotask sales process. Each column is a sales stage, and each card is one active opportunity. You can see your whole pipeline at a glance, move deals along as they progress, keep notes, and jump straight to the related quote or ticket.

Everything you do in Pipeline talks directly to Autotask. Move a card, change a date, update a probability, or add a note, and it is written back to the matching opportunity in Autotask straight away.

Before you start. You'll need the shared team password (ask your manager). Pipeline works best in a current version of Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome.

2. Signing in

Through HD IT Pulse (recommended)

Open HD IT Pulse, sign in with your Microsoft 365 account and choose Pipeline. You're signed in automatically. Access comes from your Pulse group: Pulse - Pipeline Users, or Pulse - Pipeline Admins for Settings. Pipeline matches your Microsoft 365 email to your Autotask resource; if it can't, it asks you to choose your name once (change it later from your initials menu).

With the team password

While the team password is still switched on, signing in at Pipeline's own address takes two quick steps. Choosing your name is what lets Pipeline record your notes against the right person in Autotask.

  1. Open the board's web address. You'll see the sign-in screen.
  2. Enter the team password and click Continue.
  3. Choose your name from the drop-down list. If your team has two-factor sign-in turned on, also enter the current authenticator code from LastPass. Then click Enter pipeline.

Staying signed in

Pipeline remembers you on that browser for about 12 hours. After that, or on a new device, you'll be asked for the password and your name again.

Signing out

Click your initials in the top-right corner, then choose Sign out.

Why pick your name? Pipeline uses one shared password, then asks who you are. The name you choose is stamped on every note you add, so colleagues (and Autotask) can see who recorded each update. Always select your own name.

3. The board at a glance

Columns are stages

Each column is a sales stage from your Autotask process, arranged left to right in order. Under each stage name you'll see how many opportunities are in that stage, the total one-off value sitting there, and the total monthly recurring revenue (shown as a /mo figure) where a stage has any.

Cards are opportunities

Each card shows the opportunity title and company, the one-off value with the win probability next to it (e.g. $24,656 and 50%), monthly recurring revenue if any, the projected close date, the latest human note, the quote number if a quote is linked, and a small ticket icon — highlighted (with a count if more than one) when the opportunity has a linked ticket, faded when it has none. Click a card to open its full detail.

5. Working with an opportunity

Move a deal to another stage

Drag its card into the new stage column. The opportunity's stage is updated in Autotask immediately.

Edit the details

Click a card to open the detail panel. You can change the title, description, projected close date and probability, then click Save changes. The company, owner, one-off value and monthly recurring revenue are shown for reference and are edited in Autotask itself.

Add a note

Type your update in the Add a note box and click Add note. The note is saved to Autotask under the name you signed in with, and the most recent note appears on the card.

The activity feed

The detail panel lists the most recent notes people have added. Automatic, system-generated entries are filtered out, so you only ever see real human updates.

Who gets the credit? Notes are recorded under the name you chose at sign-in. Stage moves, date changes and probability updates are saved through the integration account rather than your personal name — the change still happens, it's simply attributed to the integration.

6. Filtering and sorting

The filters work together — you can show one owner's deals, for one client, closing this week. The headline numbers always reflect the active filters.

Filter by owner / client

Use the All owners and All clients drop-downs (built automatically from the deals on the board). Choose the "All" option again to clear.

Search by title

Use the Search box at the top of the board to find an opportunity by name. As you type, the board narrows to cards whose title contains your text, and the headline counts update to match. Search combines with the owner, client and period filters, so you can search within a filtered view. Click the × in the box to clear it.

Filter by period

The Period drop-down narrows the board by projected close date. Options are grouped into By week and By month, and it opens on Current Month.

Show or hide stages

Click Stages to tick the columns you want. Your choice is remembered in that browser, and hidden stages are left out of the headline totals.

Sort a stage

Each stage header has four small buttons: $ (one-off value), % (probability), ID (opportunity ID) and Date (projected close date). Click to sort; click again to flip direction. Undated opportunities sort to the bottom. The default is value, highest first.

7. Understanding the weighted pipeline

The Weighted pipeline figure is a forecast: one-off value × probability, added up across qualifying opportunities. Points to know:

  • It counts Stage 6 and Stage 7 opportunities only — the deals closest to closing.
  • It uses one-off revenue only; monthly recurring is not included.
  • The probability comes from each opportunity's own field — a deal at 0% adds nothing.
  • It respects the Period filter.

Example: a $10,000 deal at 50% contributes $5,000, and a $4,000 deal at 25% contributes $1,000 — together $6,000 of weighted pipeline.

8. Tips and FAQ

A note I added isn't on the card

The card preview shows the latest note and updates as soon as you add one. Open the card to see the full list. Only human notes are shown — Autotask's automatic change entries are deliberately left out.

The board looks out of date

Click Refresh. If you've just had an update deployed, hard-refresh with Ctrl + Shift + R.

A column looks empty

Check the Period, Owner and Client filters — a column can look empty simply because nothing matches the current filters.

Quotes and tickets

If an opportunity has a primary quote, its number and a link appear on the card and in the detail. The detail also lists linked tickets (with a Copy button for the ticket numbers), a Create linked ticket button that opens Autotask's new-ticket screen already linked, and an Associate existing ticket button. The associate button opens a searchable picker of the opportunity's company tickets — type to filter by number or title (it shows open tickets from the last 6 months, newest first), choose one, and it's linked to the opportunity straight away, with the card and detail updating immediately.
