What's new in Pipeline

v1.13.0 current

23 October 2026
Added
  • Runs inside HD IT Pulse. Open Pipeline from Pulse and you're signed in with your Microsoft 365 account. No team password or authenticator code.
  • Access comes from your Pulse group: Pulse - Pipeline Users, or Pulse - Pipeline Admins for Settings.
  • Your Autotask name is matched from your Microsoft 365 email. If it can't be, Pipeline asks you to choose it once.
  • The team password still works at Pipeline's own address for now. Open in HD IT Pulse on the sign-in screen takes you to Pulse.

v1.12.0

24 June 2026
Changed
  • The Associate existing ticket picker is now searchable — type to filter by ticket number or title.
  • The list is tidier: completed/resolved tickets are hidden, only tickets from the last 6 months are shown, sorted newest first.

v1.11.0

24 June 2026
Changed
  • Associate existing ticket now works inside Pipeline: pick from a list of the company's tickets and it links to the opportunity straight away — no Autotask popup needed.

v1.10.0

24 June 2026
Added
  • A small ticket icon on each card shows at a glance whether an opportunity has a linked ticket (highlighted, with a count if more than one) or not (faded).
  • An Associate existing ticket button in the opportunity detail, next to "Create linked ticket".

v1.9.1

24 June 2026
Fixed
  • Sign-in now shows the real reason when a login is rejected (wrong password or wrong authenticator code) instead of a misleading "session expired" message.

v1.9.0

24 June 2026
Added
  • Optional two-factor sign-in: when enabled, the login asks for a 6-digit authenticator code (from LastPass) as well as the team password.
  • Off unless your administrator turns it on, so nothing changes for you until then.

v1.8.0

24 June 2026
Added
  • A search box in the top bar — type any part of an opportunity title and the board filters to matching cards instantly.
  • Search works together with the owner, client and period filters, and the headline totals update to match. A clear (×) button resets it.
